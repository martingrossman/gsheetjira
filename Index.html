One project. Three perspectives.

Sheet is your editable issue register. Change cells directly, or open an issue for every detail.

Kanban keeps work moving. Drag a card to change its status or reorder it. You can also change status in the issue editor, including on touch devices.

Gantt shows your schedule. Drag a bar to move its dates; drag the right edge to change its due date. Open an issue for precise dates and dependencies. Focus a row label and use Alt + ↑/↓ to reorder, Alt + ← to unparent, or Alt + → to nest under the previous eligible sibling.

Hierarchy: epics contain stories; tasks and bugs can belong to either. Choose an epic in the sidebar to focus on all its descendants.

Shortcuts: 1 / 2 / 3 switch views, / searches, N creates an issue, Esc closes an editor.

CSV export includes all fields for the currently filtered issues.
